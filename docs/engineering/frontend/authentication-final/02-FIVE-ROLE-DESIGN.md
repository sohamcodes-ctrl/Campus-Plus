## 1. Design System Philosophy
The Campus Plus visual design language conveys "premium institutional software" rather than a student project, generic SaaS template, or consumer social app.

Key tenets:
- Precise typography, balanced geometry, and generous whitespace.
- Restrained borders and subtle role-tinted surfaces.
- Zero neon colors, zero gradients, zero glassmorphism, zero AI stock graphics.

## 3. Equal Prominence & Card Geometry
All 5 persona option cards share equal visual weight and geometry:
- Distinct, semantic SVG icon in an 8x8 / 9x9 container.
- Persona title and verified short description without text truncation.
- Clear selection indicator: 2px primary border, accent background tint, and actionText checkmark.
- Full keyboard accessibility with `role="radio"`, `aria-checked`, and `aria-selected`.
