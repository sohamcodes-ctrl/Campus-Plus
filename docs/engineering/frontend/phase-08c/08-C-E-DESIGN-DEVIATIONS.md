# Phase 08-C-E: Controlled Design Deviations & Institutional Refinements
**Project:** Campus Plus — Campus Complaint & Grievance Resolution System  
**Document Type:** Design Deviation Log  
**Status:** RATIFIED & APPROVED  

---

## 1. Approved Refinements

1. **Natural Authenticated Greeting:** Rather than generic "Welcome, Student Portal", the workspace displays "Student Grievance Workspace" with authenticated email and role badge.
2. **Elimination of Auto-Closure Deadlines:** Removed unratified claim that complaints automatically close in "5 business days", replacing it with honest notification requesting verification.
3. **Desktop Table to Mobile Card Transformation:** Swapped horizontal table scrolling on small devices for optimized card list view for superior mobile ergonomics.
